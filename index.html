Will I see helper profiles after filling the form?
No. This is an assisted-enquiry website, not a live profile directory. The form prepares a message for H2W to discuss your requirements and availability.
What are the charges?
Charges depend on the role and arrangement. Ask for an itemised quote covering any agency fee, worker pay and applicable extras. No payment is collected on this website.
Is there a refund or replacement guarantee?
No blanket refund or replacement guarantee is advertised here. Ask H2W to clarify any applicable conditions and charges in writing before paying or engaging a worker.
How quickly can someone start?
Start dates depend on suitable candidates, role, schedule and location. Open 24 hours refers to business hours, not a guaranteed immediate response or placement.
Which areas do you cover?
H2W is based in Gaur City, Shahberi, Greater Noida, with a Delhi NCR service focus. Share your area or PIN code so availability can be confirmed.
Do you offer medical care?
The elder-support service listed here is non-medical. Nursing, medical procedures and emergency response are not offered through this page. Contact a qualified healthcare provider for medical needs.
What happens to the information I enter?
This page prepares the message in your browser. It does not submit the form to an H2W database. If you choose to open WhatsApp, the message is passed to WhatsApp; press Send there to share it with H2W. Avoid identity documents, bank details and health records.
